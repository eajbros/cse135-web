Why I Like Computer Architecture

Computer architecture is interesting to me because it feels a lot more tangible than high-level software. When you import things, you don't ever fully understand what's going on. But with architecture, you can see how the hardware works and how software interacts with it at a low level, having complete control over what goes on.
